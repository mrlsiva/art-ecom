About Us

Our Story

SIA Walls was born from a simple idea: that fine art should be a living part of everyday spaces, not just something to admire from afar. As an ardent art lover and active member of our local creative community, our founder Anurag Rochlani saw a unique opportunity - bridging the gap between talented artists and those who yearn for art’s uplifting impact in their homes and workplaces.

The inspiration struck while helping friends decorate a new office. The choices were either bland, mass-produced prints or making a hefty investment in art to hang on the walls forever. Why couldn’t art be more flexible—rotating, refreshing, and even interactive? Why couldn’t local artists get the recognition (and compensation) they deserve, beyond gallery walls?

And so, SIA Walls was launched, creating a new local marketplace where art could be enjoyed, swapped, or even purchased on accessible terms. We focus on original works from artists in our region, making “living with art” a dynamic part of our customers’ worlds, while supporting those who create it. SIA stands for Soul Inspired Art, by the way, as we truly believe you bring the artist's creative energy and essence onto your walls with these artworks.

What Sets Us Apart

  • Every artwork is handpicked, original, and locally sourced, typically ranging from $500 to $10,000 in value.
  • Curated Selection: Each piece is handpicked for quality, originality, and emotional impact.
  • We believe in transparent, fair partnerships that directly benefit the artists—with each rental or sale.
  • Art lovers can rotate pieces every few months, or make a favorite piece their own via purchase.

Our goal isn’t just to decorate walls—it’s to enrich lives, spur conversations, and help artists and art appreciators connect in mutually meaningful ways.

Join us in making art a lived experience, one space at a time.
